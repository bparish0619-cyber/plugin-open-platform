Display rules and supported formats

600 × 350 pixel-perfect green preview, without scaling or interpolation. Small images are centered on black; excess width or height is cropped from the right or bottom. Switching slots only changes the preview. Tap Send to transfer it to the glasses.

Supports PNG, JPEG, BMP and WebP, subject to the phone decoder. Transparency is composited on black. Imported files stay in the plugin’s private storage; the glasses keep only the current frame.
